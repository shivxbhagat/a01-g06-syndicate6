## Summary

In our iteration one retrospective, we decided to define more specific roles for each team member to ensure effective contributions. As a result, everyone had the opportunity to create their own branches to work on their respective developer tasks. We also agreed to communicate major changes before committing and continued maintaining detailed meeting notes, documenting significant changes and plans for each iteration.

## Next Steps

### Start: What should we try in the next phase?

-   Hold group meetings as early as possible to plan for the next iteration.
-   Minimize merge conflicts as much as possible.
-   Ensure the developer branch is updated as a backup of the main branch at least one to two days before the iteration deadline.

### Stop: What should we avoid in the next phase?

-   Not responding to messages promptly.
-   Not pushing to the develop branch at least one to two days before the iteration deadline.

### Continue: What should we keep doing in the next phase?

-   Holding regular TR meetings on Discord.
-   Maintaining a structured approach to work instead of rushing tasks.
-   Using GitFlow as our branching strategy.
-   Supporting group members who are struggling with a task.
-   Speaking up when a member is struggling with a task.
-   Assigning specific development task roles.
